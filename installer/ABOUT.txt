Thank you for choosing ClipVault!

ClipVault is a modern, high-performance clipboard manager designed 
for Windows 10 and 11. It extends your workflow with multi-format 
history, full persistence, intelligent deduplication, and rich data 
support.

KEY CAPABILITIES:
- Real Windows Clipboard Integration: Captures Plain Text, Rich HTML,
  Images/Screenshots, Files & Folders, and URLs.
- Win + V Inspired Flat Design: Clean, responsive list view with
  single-click instant paste and keyboard navigation.
- 100% Local-First Privacy: All history, database records, and media 
  snapshots stay strictly on your local machine. Zero telemetry.
- Backup & Migration: One-click export and import of your entire
  clipboard history and pinned items across devices.
- Auto-Start with Windows: Ready immediately every time you boot.
