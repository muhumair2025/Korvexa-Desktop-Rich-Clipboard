===================================================================
 ClipVault — Privacy Policy & Local-First Statement
 Organization: Korvexa.app (https://korvexa.app)
 Contact: support@korvexa.app
===================================================================

1. 100% LOCAL STORAGE
All clipboard history, text snippets, HTML content, image files,
and file metadata captured by ClipVault are stored exclusively on
your local storage drive in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipVault\

2. ZERO CLOUD TELEMETRY & ZERO TRACKING
ClipVault does NOT upload, transmit, sync, or broadcast your clipboard
data to any external servers, third-party analytics, or cloud networks.
The application operates entirely offline.

3. SENSITIVE DATA PROTECTION
ClipVault includes built-in heuristic pattern matching to automatically
detect and protect sensitive inputs (such as passwords, private keys, 
credit card numbers, and API tokens).

4. IGNORED APPLICATIONS
You can configure ClipVault in Settings to ignore specific security
applications (e.g. password managers like 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePass)
to prevent sensitive credentials from ever being recorded in history.

5. DATA REMOVAL & PORTABILITY
You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your
complete history via Settings > History > Export Backup, or permanently
delete all history at any time.

By installing ClipVault, you acknowledge and agree to this local-first
privacy model.
